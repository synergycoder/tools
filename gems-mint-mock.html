How minting works

01

Random draw

Your mint pulls one entry uniformly at random from whatever the owner has queued — never first-come-first-served, never previewable.

02

Sealed reveal

You receive a real token immediately, but its art and traits stay behind a shared placeholder for a short delay — long enough that no one can snipe based on the outcome.

03

Guaranteed unique

No two Gems ever share an exact (chain, shape, color, metal, status) combination — enforced on-chain, permanently, across the whole collection.

04

No supply cap

Minting stays open until the owner closes it — the only real ceiling is running out of unique trait combinations to give out.
