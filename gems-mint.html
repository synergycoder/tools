How minting works

01

Assembled on the fly

Your mint randomly draws a chain, shape, color, and metal from the on-chain rosters at the moment you mint — never previewable, never pre-curated.

02

Sealed reveal

You receive a real token immediately, but its art and traits stay behind a shared placeholder for a short delay — long enough that no one can snipe based on the outcome.

03

Guaranteed unique

If the random draw collides with a combination that's already been minted, the contract automatically retries with a new draw — no two Gems ever share an exact (chain, shape, color, metal, status) combination, enforced on-chain, permanently, across the whole collection.

04

Multiple in one transaction

Minting more than one queues that many MintAssembled calls into a single signed transaction — one Adena approval, not one per piece. Since messages in a transaction are atomic, if any one call fails (e.g. you'd exceed the per-wallet limit partway through) none of them commit.
