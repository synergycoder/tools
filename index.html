gno.tools

Read-only tools for gno.land — static, client-side, no backend. Connect Adena once in the header above and it works across every tool here.

Wallet Scanner

Connect Adena and see everything one address holds — native GNOT, every GRC20 token, and every NFT collection this scanner knows about.

Name Registry

Explore the on-chain user registry — query, browse, view registration history, and register a name.

Vanity Address

Generate a real gno.land keypair entirely in your browser, optionally searching for an address containing a chosen string.

Address Converter

Re-encode an address under any live Cosmos chain's prefix — one at a time, or a whole mixed-chain list at once with CSV export.

Batch Send

Send a payment to many recipients at once — paste a list, upload a CSV, or pull one from a public Google Sheet.
